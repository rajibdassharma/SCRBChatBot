7.how do i visit the ashram
To visit the Sri Aurobindo Ashram in Pondicherry, you can reach Pondicherry by train, bus, or car. There are frequent buses from Chennai to Pondicherry, which takes about three hours. The Ashram is located about 2 km from the Pondicherry bus stand and can be easily reached by auto-rickshaw or cycle-rickshaw. 

Once you arrive at the Ashram, there are several guest houses run by the Ashram where visitors can stay at reasonable rates. For information regarding accommodation, you can contact the Bureau Central of the Sri Aurobindo Ashram. Their contact details are as follows:
